📘 How to talk about free time activities

We use Present Simple for habits and routines:

  • I read a book on a rainy day.
  • We go surfing on a hot day.

⚠️ Note: after go + -ing (go surfing), after play + sport (play volleyball), after do + housework (do the gardening).

📘 Remember from lesson 1b (Better safe than sorry)

Should / Shouldn't = advice:

  • You should install an alarm system.
  • You shouldn't open the door to strangers.

Phrasal verbs with run:

  • run after — бежать за, преследовать: Don't run after the burglar!
  • run into — случайно встретить: I ran into my friend at the shopping centre.
  • run out of — закончиться: We ran out of milk, so we went shopping.

✏️ Exercise 8. Give advice with should / shouldn't

Read the situation and write the advice. Use the words in brackets.

  1. Your friend is crazy about skateboarding but doesn't wear a helmet.
    → You (wear a helmet).
  2. Your sister wants to go surfing on a cold day.
    → You (not go surfing / cold day).
  3. Your brother has plenty of free time but stays at home all day.
    → He (hang out with friends).
  4. Your friend is going to a theme park and wants to go on a ride without a ticket.
    → You (not go on a ride without a ticket).
  5. Your mum is tired after work.
    → She (have lunch outdoors / relax).
